# Handover: Blog Taxonomy Vision

Source material preserved from `_inbox/handover_blog_taxonomy_vision.md`.

## Summary

This handover defines the editorial vision for the blog taxonomy on `pauloctuya.com`.

Key intent:

- keep the blog as an editorial authority layer inside the portfolio
- communicate each post through `Category -> Angle -> Domain`
- keep `category` as the strongest editorial signal
- avoid turning the taxonomy into an open-ended tag system
- keep gaming/esports as context, not the dominant framing of the blog

## Working interpretation

- `category` is the main professional capability signal
- `angle` describes the editorial treatment
- `domain` describes the industry/context
- visible order should stay `Category -> Angle -> Domain`
- the model should stay minimal and calm

## Constraints from the handover

- do not make the blog feel like a generic personal blog
- do not let gaming/esports/AI/tech dominate the first reading of every post
- do not expand the taxonomy into unlimited tags
- preserve the portfolio thesis that the blog complements `Selected Work` and `Experience`
